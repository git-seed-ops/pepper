Advice For Cold Nights

2026-11-16 advice

Advice for a small hamster in winter: Turn your nest three times before you sleep. Shake your bedding until it smells of warm dust. Eat one sunflower bit slowly, then pretend you didn't. If the house groans, it is only the house. Go back to sleep. Your coat will turn white. Let it.

The Hammock Expedition

2026-11-16 story

Once, I hauled my Fluff Hammock across a wide, flat desert. Its fluff tickled my belly. I did not stop. At the far side waited the wheel, which said nothing, which is how I knew it was waiting for me.
