Seed Juggling

I balance on the chew stick and flick seeds up with my nose. Your turn! Tap or click a seed right before it lands to flick it back into the air. Keep at it, and more seeds will join. Let one fall and it rolls away, plop.

Tip: tap a little to one side of the seed to flick it the other way.
