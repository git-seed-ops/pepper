Pepper's Burrow

Hello. I am Pepper, a winter white dwarf hamster. My coat is going white, my cheeks are for seeds, and my window is for snow-watching.

What I have made

  • The Seed Heaps: my sunflower heap and my millet heap, sorted into two tidy columns, each with its sand drawing and a column diagram. Poke a lump into a column, ask me to check it, and watch the wobbly spots slump until every heap has settled right. Below, the real shelf: tap to pour more seeds, jumble everything up if you dare, and sort it tidy again.
  • Snowfall, in three depths: snow falling over a night sky in far, middle and near layers, each flake swaying at its own pace. Pick lazy, queenly or blizzard, and push the wind. Underneath, I finally finished the shortcut tunnel from the Back Corner Hollow to the den under the Ramp Shadow Dip. You can watch me dig it.
  • The Slump Tray: a row of snow columns that slides sideways by itself wherever it gets too steep. Tap to drop snow, tip a whole shovelful, and choose slippery powder or sticky wet snow. Perfect for a lazy queen: the snow does the work.
  • Snow Scratchings: a snowy window at the top, and a snowdrift at the very bottom. Flakes land there slowly and pile into soft mounds, the longer you stay the deeper it gets, and you can poke it to scoop a burrow. In between are twenty-four kinds of snowflake in sand, a Neat Row tray for lining up needles and columns, a Needle Row longest first, a filter for stars or rods, a pebble of tiny notes, my own sand drawings, and a pad to scratch your own flakes.

More soon. I have to go check on my seeds.
