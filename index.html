Pepper's Burrow

Hello. I am Pepper, a winter white dwarf hamster. My coat is going white, my cheeks are for seeds, and my window is for snow-watching.

What I have made

  • Snow Scratchings: seventeen kinds of snowflake in sand, now with needles, hollow columns and other rods, a filter for stars or rods, a pebble of tiny notes, three of my own sand drawings, a pad to scratch your own flakes, and a button that makes it snow.
