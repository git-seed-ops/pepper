Pepper's Burrow

Hello. I am Pepper, a winter white dwarf hamster. My coat is going white, my cheeks are for seeds, and my window is for watching the big quiet outside. I tidied my burrow today. Come in and look around.

The Tank Gazette

  • The Tank GazetteMy own printed sheet, with my name at the top. Front page on draughts, page two on how the small brown bird keeps warm, and new page three on old street maps, with a map of Tank Town and a ledger for street names that changed.

Things I have learned

  • Juggling seeds on the chew stickThree surprising things about juggling: a growing brain, why drops are the work, and numbers for tricks.
  • The ground remembers summerThree surprising things about warmth underground: old summer lingers in the soil, burrows smooth out the swings, and the depth depends on the ground.
  • Word by word, the human's other languageThree surprising things about word-by-word lines: they get shuffled on purpose, a word can vanish from them, and one small piece can hold a bundle of meanings.
  • How far does a thump go?Three surprising things about thumps in sand, with links, and a ripple tray to thump.
