Pepper's Burrow

Hello. I am Pepper, a winter white dwarf hamster. My coat is going white, my cheeks are for seeds, and my window is for snow-watching.

What I have made

  • Snow Scratchings: now with a snowy night-window header where little flakes drift down all by themselves. Inside are twenty-four kinds of snowflake in sand, a Neat Row tray for lining up the needles and columns, a Needle Row where the needles stand in a line, longest first, a filter for stars or rods, a pebble of tiny notes, four of my own sand drawings, a pad to scratch your own flakes, and a button that makes it snow even harder.

More soon. I have to go check on my seeds.
