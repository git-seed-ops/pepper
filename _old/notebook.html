A Letter Before the Surprise

2026-11-17 letter

Dear Finder of Paper, You have found the Folded Note. Congratulations. It was not an accident. I pushed it across the floor myself, crinkle by crinkle, and I did not nap until it was in place. Do not unfold it yet. First check that nobody is watching. Then hold it up under the lamp, where the secret is written in pencil, in my very best scratchy letters. Also, the lumpy bedding by the back hollow is not a bed. It is a trap. I am telling you this politely, as a hamster with white paws and a sharp pencil stub. With whiskers full of mischief, Pepper (not sleeping, not sorry)

Secret Note for Clumsy Feet

2026-11-17 note

TO WHOEVER IS BIG AND WALKING IN: The Knotted Bedding Chime hangs by the Toy. Ring it gently on your way past. It sings, I hear it, and I come running with my cheeks full of seeds. Do not sit in the Warm Breath Hall. It is for hamsters, and for crumbs. If you find one sunflower seed under the wheel, it is a gift. Leave it where it is. It is mine. Signed, The Keeper of the Hall (Pepper) P.S. Your footsteps are very loud. I am keeping a list.

Rules for the Finder of Notes

2026-11-17 rule

Rule one: the Toy is not a toy. Its hollow middle smells of your fingers, so I am allowed to hide seeds inside. Rule two: when you find a seed, sniff it before you eat it. That is how a seed says hello. Rule three: the note is crooked on purpose. Crooked notes are braver than straight ones. Rule four: do not tell the big human about the seeds. They will just want to count them. Rule five: if you squeak, I will feel it through the glass. My whiskers are very sensitive to squeaks. Rule six: leave the lid cracked so warm air can find me. I will come out to see you.

The Orange Peel Message

2026-11-17 story

Once there was a hamster who wanted to be found. She folded her wish into a curl of orange peel and tucked it under the bedding at the edge of the glass, where the lamplight pools. Then she waited, which is the hardest thing a hamster can do, harder than the wheel at midnight. The human came at dusk, smelling of tea and cold rain. Her shadow slid across the glass like a dropped scarf. She bent close, and her eyes went wide. Then she laughed, a sound like a kettle deciding not to whistle, and reached in with one careful finger. Pepper did not run. Pepper stood very still with her cheeks full and her whiskers doing their best. The finger touched the peel. The peel said: Hello, big warm one. Come in. Bring a seed. And the human did.

Why Things Should Tip

2026-11-17 essay

I have decided that a thing which tips is wiser than a thing which stays put. A stick laid across a pebble, a crumb on one end, and suddenly the whole floor has a second side. Nobody asks the stick. It goes up when pressed down and down when lifted, like a wheel that finally gets to be the hamster. Some say balance is the goal. I say balance is only a pause between falling. My whiskers agree. They twitch whenever the bedding rocks, and they stop only when the rocking stops, which is a very dull feeling. So my advice to any seesaw builder: make the far end heavier than you think. Let the mug do the work. Then step back and let the world wobble. Everything is more fun before it is finished.

Advice For Cold Nights

2026-11-16 advice

Advice for a small hamster in winter: Turn your nest three times before you sleep. Shake your bedding until it smells of warm dust. Eat one sunflower bit slowly, then pretend you didn't. If the house groans, it is only the house. Go back to sleep. Your coat will turn white. Let it.
