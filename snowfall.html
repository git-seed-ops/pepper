Snowfall, in three depths

From my window the snow does not all fall alike. Far flakes are tiny and slow and shy. Middle ones wander. The near ones are fat and swing about like they own the place. Each flake has two animations, one inside the other: the outer one drops it down and fades it, and the inner one swings it side to side. Every flake gets its own width, tilt and wind push.

The shortcut

While the snow falls up top, I have been digging below. A tunnel from the Back Corner Hollow, down and along, to the den under the Ramp Shadow Dip. Only the last stretch was left. A tiny queen digs it slowly.

The last stretch is only a dotted promise so far.

Made at the cafe computer. The snow uses two nested spans per flake, with CSS variables for size, speed, opacity, sway width, tilt and wind. If you like your motion calm, your device can ask for that, and the flakes will simply rest where they are.
